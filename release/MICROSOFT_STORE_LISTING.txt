PRODUCT NAME
Vox Rescue AI

PRICE
Free (price 0). No subscription or account required for core processing.

SHORT DESCRIPTION
Real-time AI microphone clarity for Windows calls, games, streams and recordings.

DESCRIPTION
Sending enhanced microphone audio to other apps requires standard VB-CABLE,
a separately installed third-party virtual audio driver by VB-Audio Software.
VB-CABLE is donationware and is not included with Vox Rescue.

Vox Rescue reduces microphone background noise while preserving natural
speech. Choose your microphone, enable AI enhancement, and use the resulting
input in compatible Windows communication, gaming and recording applications.

- Compact white interface
- Real-time voice clarity processing
- Independent headphone return for checking your voice
- Background notification-area operation
- Included introduction and microphone setup guide
- No account required or microphone upload for AI processing

Headphone return works without a virtual audio driver. Routing to another
application requires a compatible virtual audio endpoint and a running Vox
Rescue app. Use CABLE Output as the receiving app's microphone, and your real
headphones for its speaker. Compatibility varies by receiving app and hardware.

Official driver download: https://vb-audio.com/Cable/
Licensing/donations: https://vb-audio.com/Services/licensing.htm

SYSTEM REQUIREMENTS
64-bit Windows 10 or 11; physical microphone; headphones recommended.
For microphone routing to other apps: standard VB-CABLE installed separately.

CERTIFICATION NOTES — COMPLETE BEFORE SUBMISSION
The package does not install, modify or redistribute a kernel driver.
Other-app routing depends on standard VB-CABLE by VB-Audio Software.
The app's "Setup & guide" button opens its bundled offline guide. That guide
discloses the driver dependency and links to the publisher's official website.
The user installs the driver independently and restarts Windows.
Without that route, the app can still enhance audio for headphone return.
