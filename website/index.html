Vox Rescue AI

Real-time AI voice clarity for Windows

Information

Vox Rescue AI is a microphone enhancement application for Windows. It reduces background noise while preserving natural speech, so your voice is easier to hear in calls, games, streams and recordings.

The processed microphone can be selected in compatible applications such as Discord, Microsoft Teams, Zoom, OBS Studio and games that let you choose a Windows audio input.

Features:

  • Real-time noise reduction
  • Natural speech preservation
  • AI enhancement switch
  • Headphone return
  • Virtual microphone routing
  • Compact white interface
  • Notification-area operation
  • No account required

AI enhancement turns voice processing on or off.
Hear my return lets you check the result through headphones.
Setup & guide explains how to use the microphone in other apps.

Supported operating systems: 64-bit Windows 10 / 11.

Routing to other applications uses standard VB-CABLE, installed separately from VB-Audio Software. It is third-party donationware and is not bundled with Vox Rescue. Headphone return works without this driver.

Help

Choose your microphone, enable AI enhancement and select the processed input in the app where you want to use it.

01

Choose your devices

Open Vox Rescue and choose your physical microphone under Microphone. Choose your headphones under Headphones / return.

Wait for the engine to load, then switch AI enhancement on. Use Hear my return when you want to listen to the result. Headphones help prevent feedback.

02

Set up the microphone route

To send your voice to another app, install standard VB-CABLE from its official publisher, follow the installation instructions and restart Windows.

Reopen Vox Rescue or press Refresh. In the receiving application, select CABLE Output as its microphone. Keep its speaker/output on your real headphones.

03

Calls, games & recording

Discord: User Settings → Voice & Video → Input Device → CABLE Output.
Teams / Zoom: choose CABLE Output in microphone settings.
Games: choose CABLE Output in the voice input selector when supported.
OBS: add an Audio Input Capture source and choose CABLE Output.

Keep Vox Rescue running. Make a short microphone test before a call, match or stream. Read the full introduction and setup guide.

FAQ

Q: Is Vox Rescue free?
A: The app is intended to be free, with no subscription or account required for its core voice features. Public release information is on the download page.

Q: Does my microphone audio need to be uploaded?
A: AI processing runs on your PC. The communication or recording app receiving your voice has its own privacy practices.

Q: Why is there no voice in Discord or my game?
A: Keep Vox Rescue running, check the physical microphone selected in Vox Rescue, and choose CABLE Output in the receiving app. Check Windows microphone permissions and confirm the virtual route is available.

Q: Can I use it in every application?
A: It is designed for Windows apps that accept the virtual microphone as an input. Some games and devices have restrictions, so test your chosen app first.

Q: Why do I hear myself twice?
A: Switch off Hear my return or the receiving app's input monitoring. In OBS, avoid capturing the same microphone both globally and through a scene source.

Q: How do I close the app completely?
A: Closing the window keeps the app in the notification area when tray support is available. Use Exit in the window or tray menu to stop it.
