What is measured

Vox Rescue is evaluated across everyday speech, difficult background noise, reverberation and interfering sound. DNS Challenge 5 is the published training and evaluation foundation for the product.

The public performance description intentionally focuses on user-facing behavior. Exact results can change with the microphone, Windows driver, selected audio route, computer and the other voice processing enabled in the receiving app.

Vox Rescue publishes the conditions and product behavior it can stand behind. It does not turn one computer's result into a universal promise.
