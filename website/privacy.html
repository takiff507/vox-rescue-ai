Privacy

Your voice stays yours.

Vox Rescue is designed to give you control over the microphone and applications that use it.

Short version: Vox Rescue uses the microphone and output devices you choose and does not require an account for its main voice features.

1. Microphone audio

Vox Rescue uses the microphone and output devices you select to provide voice clarity and optional return monitoring. It does not make a recording of your conversation as part of normal use.

2. Product website

This website is made from static HTML, CSS, JavaScript and image files. It contains no advertising network, analytics SDK, tracking pixel, social widget, third-party font or account system.

The JavaScript file handles navigation, updates the copyright year and prepares support email drafts. The Contact form does not submit your details to a website server or store them. Selecting Open email draft passes the draft to your chosen email app; review it before sending.

3. Hosting records

The hosting provider may process ordinary technical records such as IP address, requested page, browser information and timestamp to deliver and protect the website. The hosting provider's own infrastructure privacy terms apply to those records.

4. Device permissions

The Windows application needs microphone and audio-device permission to provide its stated features. You can review and change those permissions in Windows settings.

5. Third-party virtual audio route

Vox Rescue can open the official VB-CABLE website when you choose to install the driver. Vox Rescue does not send microphone audio to VB-Audio. Visiting that external website is subject to VB-Audio's own privacy and website terms.

6. Future features

If a future release adds accounts, payments, licensing, crash reporting or online services, this policy will be updated before those features are enabled.

7. Policy changes

Material changes will be posted on this page with a new update date. Contact support at hello@ksnxrl.dev.
